# 0.14: graphics, sound, controls and three WPs

The full mapped Großräschen loop is cut into three equal-distance sections of the existing smoothed road centerline. The world coordinate frame remains shared. Adjacent endpoints match, all three cover the original loop without shortcuts, and WP3 finishes at the original pin. Each WP has its own overview, timing/record key and ghost filename. WP1 retains the original sealed start; WPs2/3 do not acquire an artificial asphalt start. Seven minutes is a driving target, not forced by the clock.

Near pines now have a 3D trunk and spatial clusters of photographed needle cards, instanced into 128 m chunks with a visibility distance. Far trees use billboards; broadleaf trees still use billboards. Pine bases and other props query Terrain3D's actual height when available; root feet are buried slightly to hide sampling gaps. New photographed gravel and forest-floor diffuse/normal/roughness maps replace the largest flat terrain surfaces. Directional light and distance haze are retuned. This remains below the requested CarX/Unreal reference; procedural vehicle meshes and sparse background detail still need authored assets.

A real CC0 recording supplies the engine loop and follows RPM/throttle with smoothing. No vehicle-specific or B230 authenticity claim. Mechanical/gravel/skid layers remain procedural. Engine synthesis is retained only as fallback. Audio players release streams on scene exit.

Default input: analog virtual wheel gesture, calibrated tilt option, buttons option, adjustable sensitivity and optional automatic throttle. A critically damped steering filter smooths release and reversals; physical steering remains separate. Touch roles stay with the initial finger contact when a finger leaves its button region. RR3-identical handling is not claimed.

Headless checks cover route coverage/boundaries, isolated WP best-time keys, recorded WAV/loop import, continuous centering response, analog drag and presence of spatial tree geometry, plus the existing physics/game suites. Mobile/Vulkan screenshots use desktop llvmpipe and do not prove phone performance. Android preview retains its original debug signer; not a Store release.

For Godot headless tests, audio streams are imported and checked but not played, avoiding Godot Dummy-driver shutdown leaks. This is disclosed rather than treated as measured phone audio behavior. Actual Android audio/output and tilt calibration remain device acceptance checks.
